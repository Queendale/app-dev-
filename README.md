# My Favorite Movie: Marvel

## About Marvel

Marvel movies are my favorite because they have amazing superheroes, exciting action scenes, and interesting stories.

### My Favorite Superheroes

- **Iron Man** – A genius who builds a powerful suit.
- **Spider-Man** – A brave hero who protects people.
- **Thor** – The mighty God of Thunder.
- **Captain America** – A hero who fights for justice.
- **Hulk** – A superhero with incredible strength.

### Why I Like Marvel Movies

I like Marvel movies because:
1. They have exciting battles.
2. The superheroes have unique powers.
3. They teach us about friendship and teamwork.
4. The stories are entertaining.

### My Rating

**Rating: 10/10** ⭐
